## How the copy is built, and the one thing still yours to fill in

Every post and the video's cold open are built on the same three beats — **hook** (a claim
they don't believe), **the turn** (held seats get released 1–3 hours before showtime,
silently), then **the product as the obvious consequence**. Beat two is the one that
travels; it's worth sharing with no product attached, which is precisely why the posts work.

The copy carries concrete details in `[square brackets]` — a film, a screen, a night you
missed. **Swap them for what actually happened to you.** A real Friday beats a
better-written invented one, and inventing one is the fastest way to get taken apart in
the comments.

Two audiences, and the copy switches between them: the person who *missed out* (pitch:
sold out isn't final), and the person who *won't settle for the wrong screen* (pitch: you
don't want a seat, you want that seat in that room). Lead with the second on film and fan
channels — they get it instantly and they're the ones who stay.

## The cards

| File | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `cards/og-1200x630.png` | 1200×630 | X/LinkedIn link preview, the repo's social preview, Product Hunt gallery |
| `cards/square-1080x1080.png` | 1080×1080 | Instagram feed, LinkedIn, Discord embeds |
| `cards/vertical-1080x1920.png` | 1080×1920 | Stories, Reels/Shorts cover, WhatsApp status |

Rebuild after editing any of the HTML:

```sh
promo/cards/build.sh
```

Headless Chrome at 2× then resampled down — the same approach as `icons/build.sh`. The
hall is generated by `hall.js` from a fixed seed, so a rebuild produces the identical
layout rather than quietly reshuffling every card.

They share `card.css`, whose palette is copied from `ui.css`: the green is the extension's
`--open`, the slate is `--free`, the pale grey is `--taken`. The point is that a card in a
timeline and the popup in the video read as the same product.

**Set the OG card as the repo's social preview** — GitHub → Settings → General → Social
preview → upload `og-1200x630.png`. Every link to the repo picks it up.

## What's missing, and only you can make it

**A screenshot of the + button on a real BookMyShow listing.** Every asset in here is
rendered — real UI, but composed. The one image that proves the extension exists is your +
sitting on a live listing page, and it's also the strongest thing you can lead the store
listing with. Capture it at 1280×800 on a day when a few shows are genuinely greyed out.

Same goes for the demo video's money shot: a real late release, caught live. See the last
section of `demo-script.md`.

## Order of operations

1. Replace the `[bracketed]` story details with your own.
2. Capture the live + screenshot; make it the store listing's first screenshot (replacing
   or preceding `store/screenshot-1.png`, whose data is invented — see `store/listing.md`).
3. Record the demo. Upload the master to YouTube; the Chrome Web Store's video field takes
   a YouTube URL, not a file.
4. Add the store badge line to the top of the root `README.md`, and the store URL to the
   repo's About field.
5. Work the sequence at the end of `launch-copy.md`.

## The thing to remember after launch week

Launch week gets you one day per channel. **Opening weekends get you one every month.** The
pitch writes itself on a Friday when every good screen is already grey, and reposting the
PSA version then costs nothing. Set a reminder for the next three big releases.
